## 3D models, textures, environment

**All original, generated by this project.** Every engine is built
procedurally at runtime from its published architecture (bore, stroke,
cylinder count, bank angles, firing order). Textures (carbon weave, wrinkle
paint, cast metal, heat-shield dimples) are generated on a canvas at runtime.
The studio lighting environment is made of procedural Lightformers — no HDRI
files. No model, texture or file was taken from games (BeamNG.drive, Forza,
Gran Turismo, Assetto Corsa…), from manufacturers, or from asset stores.

## Dashboards and car silhouettes

Original SVG drawings. The instrument clusters are *inspired by* the real
clusters of each car (layout, scales, colours, documented in the research
file) and recreated as functional vector instruments; no photograph of a
dashboard is used. Car side profiles are hand-drawn original silhouettes.

## Sound

Synthesised in real time with the Web Audio API (no recordings, no samples).

## Technical data

Engine and vehicle data come from the sources listed per engine in
[`docs/engine-research.md`](docs/engine-research.md) and in the ENGINE SPECS
panel of the app (manufacturer newsrooms and technical documents first).
